Geotechnical Execution

How I produce geotech reports.

This is exactly how I work through a soil investigation report — from raw field data to final client-ready deliverable.

01

Field Data Review & Boring Log Interpretation

Review driller's field logs, SPT blow counts, and sample recovery notes from boring operations
Classify soil samples using USCS/AASHTO based on visual description and lab test results
Prepare boring logs with proper depth intervals, soil descriptions, SPT N-values, and groundwater data
Construct soil profiles and cross-sections for the investigation area
02

Laboratory Testing & Analysis

Conduct or review classification tests: sieve analysis, Atterberg Limits, moisture content, unit weight
Perform or review strength tests: unconfined compressive strength, direct shear, CBR, consolidation
Tabulate all laboratory results and cross-reference with field SPT data
Flag any anomalies between field and laboratory results before analysis proceeds
03

Slope Stability & Liquefaction Analysis

Input soil parameters into GEO5 for slope stability analysis; compute Factors of Safety for critical and non-critical failure surfaces
Perform liquefaction potential assessment using NovoLiq and NovoSPT based on SPT N-values and site seismic parameters
Use MathCAD to compute bearing capacity, allowable soil bearing pressure, and settlement estimates
Document all computations with full traceability — every input, assumption, and output recorded
04

Report Writing & Client Delivery

Prepare comprehensive Soil Investigation Report: executive summary, methodology, field and lab results, analysis, and recommendations
Include all appendices: boring logs, laboratory test results, computation sheets, and GEO5 output plots
Present findings clearly with foundation type recommendations appropriate for the site conditions
Process client review comments and issue revised final report within agreed timeline
